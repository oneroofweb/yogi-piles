Our Specialized Treatments

At Yogipiles & Panchkarma Center, we combine the ancient wisdom of Ayurveda with the precision of modern technology to offer safe, effective, and minimally invasive treatments for anorectal disorders. Every procedure is carefully selected based on the patient’s condition, lifestyle, and recovery goals.

Laser Surgery for Piles, Fissure & Fistula

A minimally invasive, painless technique using precision laser energy to remove or shrink affected tissues. It ensures quick recovery, less bleeding, and short hospital stay — often performed as a daycare procedure.

Ksharsutra Therapy

An Ayurvedic para-surgical treatment ideal for fistula-in-ano. A medicated thread is inserted into the tract, which gradually cuts, cleans, and heals it naturally. This method is highly effective with low recurrence, making it a gold standard in Ayurveda.

Ayurvedic Herbal Treatment

Customized internal and external herbal formulations help reduce inflammation, control bleeding, relieve constipation, and promote tissue healing. These remedies are side-effect-free and support long-term gut health.

Panchkarma Detox Therapy

Procedures like Basti (medicated enema) and Virechana (therapeutic purgation) are used to detoxify the digestive system. Panchkarma strengthens the colon and prevents recurrence of anorectal issues.

Anal Dilation & Fissure Management

For chronic anal fissures, we use a combination of anal dilation, soothing Ayurvedic ointments, and muscle relaxants to reduce spasms and promote painless healing — avoiding the need for surgical intervention in many cases.

Diet & Lifestyle Counseling

Since digestive health plays a major role in anorectal disorders, we provide personalized diet plans, lifestyle guidance, and daily routines based on Ayurvedic principles to support faster healing and prevent recurrence.
